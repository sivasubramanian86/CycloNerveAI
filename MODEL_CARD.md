## 2. Model Details

- **Foundation Model:** Gemini 3.7 Flash (`gemini-3.7-flash`) via the modern `@google/genai` TypeScript SDK.
- **Task Domains:**
  1. Multimodal fusion: Correlating satellite SAR flood masks, wind telemetry, and field photos into an authoritative briefing.
  2. Natural language explanation: Explaining mathematical factor contributions ($H, E, V, C$) to Incident Commanders without modifying computed scores.
  3. Multilingual synthesis: Translating complex emergency advisories into regional languages (Hindi, Telugu, Odia) preserving urgent actionable directives.
- **Safety Verifier:**
  - Every draft generated by the model is passed through the deterministic `SafetyVerifier`.
  - Rejects draft texts containing unverified casualty counts, out-of-boundary districts, or non-actionable speculation.
  - Enforces a strict **one retry maximum**. If the model fails verification twice or the API times out, the system automatically falls back to pre-approved deterministic templates.

## 3. Quantitative Risk Formulation (Symbolic)

The composite risk score is strictly deterministic:
$$\text{Composite Risk} = H \times E \times V \times C$$

Where:
- **Hazard ($H$):** Function of central barometric pressure deficit $\Delta P = 1013 - P_{c}$, sustained wind speed, and coastal storm surge overtopping height.
- **Exposure ($E$):** Population density in hazard zone and asset replacement valuation.
- **Vulnerability ($V$):** Asset structural elevation deficit relative to surge peak, physical construction material, and lack of flood barriers.
- **Criticality ($C$):** Downstream topological centrality index computed from the infrastructure dependency graph (in-degree / out-degree fan-out).

## 4. Intended Use & Boundaries

### Intended Use
- Assisting State and District Emergency Operations Centers (EOCs) during pre-landfall planning (T-72h to T-0h).
- Anticipatory staging of mobile high-capacity dewatering pumps, backup generator fuel, and emergency medical personnel.
- Drafting bilingual and multilingual public safety advisories for review by authorized Incident Commanders.

### Out-of-Scope / Prohibited Uses
- Autonomous dissemination of public emergency broadcasts without human-in-the-loop commander sign-off.
- Speculative casualty estimations or mortality forecasts.
- Use as a sole decision-maker for life-safety critical actions without physical ground verification.

## 5. Fail-Safe Mechanisms & Kill Switch

1. **Global AI Kill Switch:** An Incident Commander or Administrator can immediately engage the kill switch via the secure API or UI (`/api/system/kill-switch`). All Generative AI calls are halted immediately, and the system operates exclusively on verified deterministic heuristics.
2. **Degraded Mode:** In low-bandwidth or disconnected states (`TIER_3_AIRGAPPED_EDGE`), local cached rule engines provide continuous situational awareness without external API dependencies.
